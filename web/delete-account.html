Delete Your Starpage Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you've decided to delete your account, you can do so directly within the app or by submitting a request via email.

What happens when you delete your account?
  • Your profile, photos, videos, and posts will be permanently removed.
  • Your messages and interactions will be deleted from our active databases.
  • This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

For the fastest results, use the Starpage mobile app:

  1. Open the Starpage app.
  2. Go to your Profile tab.
  3. Tap on Settings (gear icon).
  4. Select Account Security or Manage Account.
  5. Tap Delete Account and follow the prompts to confirm.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team.

Please send an email from the address associated with your account to:

support@starpage.me

Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data Retention

Consistent with our Privacy Policy, some data may be retained for a limited period in our secure backups (up to 90 days) or as required by law for financial or legal record-keeping.

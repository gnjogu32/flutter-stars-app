Child Safety Standards & Anti-CSAE Policy

Starpage Community Safety Policy | Last Updated: October 12, 2026
Zero Tolerance Policy: Starpage strictly prohibits any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), grooming, or endangerment of minors. Any account found creating, sharing, or facilitating such content will be permanently banned immediately and reported to relevant law enforcement agencies and NCMEC.

1. Principles & Standard Policies

Starpage ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting children and ensuring a safe environment across our platform. We strictly adhere to global child protection laws, including the U.S. Protection of Children from Sexual Predators Act and European Union child safety regulations.

  • Minimum Age Requirement: Starpage is strictly for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly permit children under 13 to create accounts.
  • Prohibition of Exploitation: Content depicting, describing, promoting, or encouraging CSAM/CSAE is strictly banned.
  • Anti-Grooming Protocols: Any user interactions or messaging intended to exploit, manipulate, or endanger minors are prohibited.

2. Reporting Mechanisms & Immediate Actions

We provide accessible reporting tools across our mobile application and web interfaces:

  • In-App Reporting: Every post, reel, comment, and user profile includes a dedicated "Report" button allowing users to flag inappropriate content instantly.
  • Direct Safety Email: Urgent reports regarding child safety can be sent directly to our dedicated safety team at childsafety@starpage.me.

3. Law Enforcement & NCMEC Escalation

When CSAM/CSAE or minor endangerment content is detected or reported:

  1. The content is immediately removed from public view and quarantined.
  2. The associated user account is permanently terminated and hardware IDs banned.
  3. In compliance with federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), Starpage files mandatory reports with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies globally.

4. Moderation & Technology Enforcement

  • Proactive Review: Automated media hash matching and metadata filtering are employed to intercept prohibited content before distribution.
  • Human Safety Team: Reports flagged under child safety categories receive top-priority review by trained moderation staff.
